  \textbf{Narayana Number} \\
  \begin{Large}
    ${\displaystyle \operatorname {N} (n,k)={\frac {1}{n}}{n \choose k}{n \choose k-1}}$
  \end{Large} \\
  n balanced-parentheses with k distinct nestings, number of paths from (0,0) to (2n,0) with NE and SE directions (dyck paths/catalan with length n and exactly k peaks. More generally, it can be shown that the Narayana triangle is symmetric $N\left(n,k\right) = N\left(n,n-k+1\right)$. The sum of the rows equal the catalan number $\Sigma N\left(n,k\right) = C_n$ with $C_n$ is a catalan number.
\end{large} \\
