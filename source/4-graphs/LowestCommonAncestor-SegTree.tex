\begin{verbatim}
Let LCA(r,u,v) be the LCA of u and v with respect to
root r. To compute LCA(r,u,v), we can compute, for any
fixed ro (usually let ro = 1),
LCA(ro,r,u);    // LCA(1,r,u);
LCA(ro,r,v);    // LCA(1,r,v);
LCA(ro,u,v);    // LCA(1,u,v);
and take the "odd man out", i.e., if two are equal and
the third are different, then take the third, else if
they are all equal, take any one.
\end{verbatim}
